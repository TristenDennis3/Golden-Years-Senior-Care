About Us

Our Mission

The mission of Golden Years Senior Care is to provide compassionate, person-centered care that promotes independence, dignity, safety, and quality of life for every resident. We are committed to creating a welcoming environment where residents receive personalized support while maintaining their individuality, choices, and independence.

Our Vision

The vision of Golden Years Senior Care is to be recognized as a trusted provider of exceptional assisted living services by delivering compassionate care, maintaining high professional standards, and continuously improving services to meet the evolving needs of residents and their families.

Resident Rights

Golden Years Senior Care recognizes and protects the rights of all residents. Residents are treated with respect, dignity, fairness, and without discrimination. Residents have the right to:

  • Participate in decisions regarding their care.
  • Maintain privacy and confidentiality.
  • Receive respectful and appropriate services.
  • Be informed about facility policies and services.
